# E031 preregistration: route-family portability of a frozen Function

Date: 2026-09-21, before E031 outcomes.

E027 found a weak benefit of E026's frozen admitted Function, concentrated in one dual-mux XOR task. E028--E030 showed that direct Function use and Function-derived trajectory replacement were neither significant nor necessary on that same truth table. Stop iterating on that truth table. Test whether the frozen Function changes exact-search success across a prospectively defined family of unseen route-selection targets, with equally priced random Functions and a one-hop propagation barrier.

Freeze E026's single admitted Function without refitting. Define three route-selection tasks before running: `route_dual_mux_xnor` (XNOR of two within-half muxes), `route_cross_mux_xor` (XOR of two cross-wired muxes), and `route_cascade_mux` (a mux whose true branch is another mux). Define two precision/numeric controls: `numeric_popcount_bit1` (the second bit of six-bit population count) and `numeric_unsigned_sum_ge6` (two three-bit unsigned numbers sum to at least six). Confirm all five truth signatures are distinct from E024's probe/evaluation targets and the original E019 task signatures.

Use fresh seeds 1450--1457. For every seed/task compare four conditions: `no_transfer`, the frozen `admitted` Function, `one_hop_barrier` for that Function, and `random_matched`. Random Functions are generated once from the same expression tree and LUT codes with randomized input leaves, rejecting existing tasks, inputs, prior random signatures, and all E031 targets; they have exactly the same primitive/routing/depth cost. The one-hop barrier permits round-1 children but forbids Function-containing descendants as parents from round 2 onward, while allowing a clean expression with the same signature. Run 160 searches total at beam 128, six rounds and tree cost at most 16 with E026's fixed selector. Do not alter targets, conditions, seed count, random draws, budget, or stopping after observing results.

Primary outcome is, per seed, the number of exact route tasks solved by `admitted` minus `random_matched`. Call route-family portability supported only if the mean gain is at least +0.5 task/seed, the 20,000-resample paired bootstrap 95% interval excludes zero, and at least two of three route tasks each have at least one admitted-only exact result whose final expression uses the admitted Function. This pilot is powered for breadth rather than a small effect; failure does not prove no effect.

Secondary outcomes are admitted versus no transfer and admitted versus barrier for route-task exact counts; the corresponding numeric-control comparisons; per-task exact McNemar tests with Holm correction across all three pairwise contrasts; unbiased variance, bootstrap interval, Cohen dz, exact sign-flip p, best error, solution cost, Function use, and runtime. The route/numeric interaction is the paired difference of their per-task success-rate effects for admitted versus random, reported descriptively with a bootstrap interval.

Stop after the fixed 160 searches regardless of outcome. Re-evaluate every exact expression on all 64 inputs. Save the protocol, task signatures, fixed and random Function manifests, raw records, source hashes, audit and report under E031. This is a Boolean beam-search portability test. It does not demonstrate autonomous concept formation, description compression, physical gate reduction, acceleration, State, or learned Router behavior.
