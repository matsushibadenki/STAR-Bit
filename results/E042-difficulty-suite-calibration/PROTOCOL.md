# E042 preregistration: frozen mixed-difficulty suite calibration

Date: 2026-09-28, before E042 outcomes. E040–E041 showed that individual truth tables switch sharply between 0/6 and 6/6 across a round boundary, making repeated single-task middle-band selection unstable. E042 tests a new evaluation unit: a frozen suite containing previously calibrated easy and hard tasks. This is an independent-seed, baseline-only calibration. It does not evaluate Function transfer or inspect any Function condition.

Freeze four exact-numeric tasks: easy `numeric_2bit_sum_ge3` (E040) and `numeric_4bit_weighted_ge4` (E041), hard `numeric_rotated_weight_ge6` (E039) and `numeric_5bit_weighted_ge5` (E041). Freeze four routing tasks: easy `route_mux_xor_bit` and `route_mux_conditional_flip` (E041), hard `route_rotated_cross_xnor` and `route_nested_mux` (E040). These are the first two suitable registry entries in each available stratum; retain all eight regardless of E042 outcomes.

Use fresh seeds 1570–1581, no Library, beam 128, four rounds, maximum tree cost 16, for 96 searches. Primary metrics are the per-seed exact proportions over the four numeric tasks and over the four routing tasks. The suite-feasibility gate requires each family mean in `[0.25, 0.75]`, every seed to solve between one and three of four tasks in each family, and all runs to reach round two with realized width 128. Passing freezes the full suite for a later independent intervention experiment; it does not permit selecting individual E042 tasks.

Report per-family suite exact mean/unbiased variance, 20,000-resample bootstrap 95% CI, the paired numeric-minus-routing difference with Cohen dz and exact sign-flip p, per-task exact and best-error mean/unbiased variance, elapsed time, generated signatures, and successful circuit primitive/routing/depth costs. As descriptive sensitivity analysis, compare round 4 minus round 2 exact for all eight tasks, with exact sign-flip p and Holm correction. Re-evaluate every exact expression on all 64 inputs. Verify 96 unique records, strict JSON, progress equality, source hashes, exact suite membership, no Library signatures, round-prefix values, and width 128. Stop after 96 searches or 15 minutes of summed search time, preserving incomplete state. Do not add tasks or seeds from outcomes.
